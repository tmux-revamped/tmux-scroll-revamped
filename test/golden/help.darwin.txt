$ scroll-revamped help
scroll-revamped - the mouse wheel to apps that scroll, copy-mode for the rest

usage:
  scroll-revamped apply     bind the wheel now
  scroll-revamped list      print every binding apply would make
  scroll-revamped check ALT MOUSE HISTORY PANE
                            decide one wheel tick on tmux before 2.0: exits 0
                            to pass it to the app, 1 to enter copy-mode
  scroll-revamped doctor    check this machine
  scroll-revamped version   print the version
  scroll-revamped help      print this page

options:
  --json           list prints one JSON object
  --theme NAME     the look: kodougu, catppuccin-mocha, catppuccin-latte,
                   dracula, nord, gruvbox, tokyo-night or mono
  --SETTING VALUE  any tmux option below, named with dashes, such as
                   --wheel on or --speed 3

environment:
  SCROLL_REVAMPED_SETTING  any tmux option below in capitals, such as
                           SCROLL_REVAMPED_WHEEL
  SCROLL_REVAMPED_THEME    the look, when --theme is not given
  SCROLL_REVAMPED_ACCENT   a colour such as #CBA6F7 for the accent
  KODOUGU_THEME            the family look, when neither is given
  KODOUGU_ACCENT           the family accent, when the tool's is unset
  NO_COLOR                 turn colour off

tmux options:
  @scroll_revamped_wheel                  on routes the wheel (off)
  @scroll_revamped_passthrough_alternate  pass the wheel to an app on the
                                          alternate screen (on)
  @scroll_revamped_passthrough_mouse      pass the wheel to an app that asked
                                          for the mouse (on)
  @scroll_revamped_skip_empty             on keeps a pane with no scrollback
                                          out of copy-mode (off)
  @scroll_revamped_select_pane            on focuses the pane under the
                                          pointer first (off)
  @scroll_revamped_alt_keys               arrow or page sends keys to a full
                                          screen app without mouse (none)
  @scroll_revamped_mouse                  off leaves tmux's mouse option
                                          alone (on)
  @scroll_revamped_speed                  lines per copy-mode tick (none)
  @scroll_revamped_granularity            line, halfpage or page (line)
  @scroll_revamped_auto_exit              on leaves copy-mode at the bottom
                                          (off)
  @scroll_revamped_indicator              on flashes the scroll position (off)
  @scroll_revamped_drag_copy              on copies a mouse selection to the
                                          clipboard (off)
  @scroll_revamped_click_copy             on copies the word on a double click
                                          and the line on a triple click to
                                          the clipboard (off)
  @scroll_revamped_smooth                 on scrolls a copy-mode tick one line
                                          at a time (off)
  @scroll_revamped_status_wheel           on switches windows over the status
                                          line (off)
  @revamped_theme                         the family look (kodougu)

notes:
  The entry point runs apply once when tmux loads the plugin, and every
  binding goes to tmux in one call. On tmux 2.0 and later the wheel is decided
  by a format with no process; before 2.0 each tick runs check, which caches
  its last decision in @scroll_revamped_cache_key and _cache_val.

examples:
  scroll-revamped list
  scroll-revamped --wheel on --speed 3 apply
  scroll-revamped doctor
exit 0
